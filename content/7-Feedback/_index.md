## Sharing and Feedback

Below are my personal reflections on the experience of participating in the First Cloud AI Journey internship program. As the internship is still ongoing, this content represents a mid-term perspective rather than a final summary.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The working environment at FCAJ is quite friendly and open. When encountering issues, I could always ask without feeling judged. Communication channels are well-organized, making it easy to find documentation and announcements. However, since work is primarily remote, there can occasionally be a lack of personal connection – adding coffee chats or occasional meetups would strengthen team bonding.

**2. Mentorship and Administrative Support**

This is the most impressive aspect of the program. When I got stuck, my mentor didn't give direct answers but instead guided me on what to check or suggested relevant documentation. This approach takes more time initially but helps build long-term retention and confidence in troubleshooting. Administrative support was also solid – account setup, credits, and access permissions were all handled promptly without disrupting the learning process.

**3. Alignment Between Tasks and Academic Background**

Weekly tasks aligned well with and expanded upon knowledge from my university courses. The program's strength lies in not stopping at theory but connecting it with hands-on AWS operations, helping me see how networking and system concepts apply in real cloud environments.

**4. Skill Development Opportunities**

I learned a great deal, not just technical skills but also documentation writing, worklog management with Markdown/Hugo, blog translation, and cloud cost management. The "learn by doing" methodology through practical labs helped me retain knowledge far better than reading theory alone.

**5. Culture and Team Spirit**

The culture at FCAJ is quite positive – everyone respects each other and freely shares knowledge regardless of seniority. Even as an intern, my input was valued. The shared passion for learning and cloud technology was very motivating.

**6. Intern Support Policies**

The program provides a clear 12-week roadmap, AWS credits for labs without cost concerns, and flexible scheduling to balance with university coursework. Lab materials are well-prepared, detailed enough yet challenging enough to require active learning.

### Additional Questions

- **What was the most _satisfying_ part of the internship?**
  The most satisfying realization was that the gap between theory and practice has narrowed significantly. Before, I knew service names and understood basic concepts, but after completing the labs, I could clearly see the role of each component and how they connect in a real system.

- **What do you think the program should _improve_ for future interns?**
  Adding small group projects (2-3 people) after the foundational phase would simulate real-world collaboration. Additionally, compiling common lab errors (IAM permissions, incorrect routes, insufficient policies) into an FAQ or checklist would save significant time.

- **Would you _recommend this internship_ to your peers? Why?**
  Yes, but I would be upfront about expectations. It's an excellent program for those pursuing cloud careers who need a serious practice environment. However, it's not a light learning experience – participants need to be quite proactive to fully benefit from the program.

### Suggestions & Expectations

- **Mid-term progress reviews:** A short review session between mentor and intern to understand current progress and whether the learning direction is on track.
- **Content prioritization:** Some weeks have heavy workloads. Clearly marking which content is mandatory versus optional would make prioritization easier.
- **Team-based mini-projects:** After foundational content, a small exercise for 2-3 people to design and deploy architecture together would be more realistic.
- **Certification prep support:** Since many participants aim for AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate after the program, a session on exam strategies and study resources would be very valuable.
